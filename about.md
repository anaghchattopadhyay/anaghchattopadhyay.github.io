I am a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate in the [Department of Biostatistics](https://publichealth.jhu.edu/departments/biostatistics), Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health, working with [Nilanjan Chatterjee](https://malonecenter.jhu.edu/people/nilanjan-chatterjee). My research focuses on statistical genetics and causal inference, with an emphasis on developing novel Mendelian randomization methods that improve instrument selection and validity. I am particularly interested in applications to maternal and perinatal health outcomes, breast cancer risk prediction, and complex trait analysis across diverse populations. I also work on interpretable AI/ML methods.
